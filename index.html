🚂 HO Layout Designer
Click a red track end to select it (turns green), then click parts below to keep laying track. F flips the last part.
Pick an item, then click on a track to place it. It snaps to the nearest track. Esc to stop.
Pick an item, then click on the board. Keep clicking to place more; Esc to stop.
Or draw any shape: click corners, double-click to finish. In edit mode drag corners, double-click an edge to add a corner, right-click a corner to delete it.
3D view needs an internet connection to load three.js.
